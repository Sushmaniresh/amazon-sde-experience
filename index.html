🖥️Loop format

  • 4 × 60 min on Zoom. LiveCode: no autocomplete, can't run code
  • They won't say which round is what, so prep everything
  • Ask to split it over 2 days. I did, it really helped
Interviewing at Amazon ↗

🎤Mocks: my biggest fix

I used to freeze in mocks when I didn't know the answer. Doing mocks regularly fixed that. You don't need the full answer, just start.

And it worked. I solved one graph question and almost finished the other in my loop, and now I feel confident going into any interview.

✅Top tips

  1. Give lots of mocks. Start even if unsure
  2. Graph templates cold: BFS, DFS, cycles
  3. LP story doc, get grilled on follow-ups
  4. Expect the question to change mid-way. Clarify early
  5. Be confident. You prepared for this
